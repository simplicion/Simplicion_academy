    Design Systems

Why this matters:

If you later add AI tutors, they can identify which competency a learner is weak in, not just which lesson they haven't watched.

Your platform can recommend the next module based on missing competencies rather than static prerequisites.

This small architectural decision now will make the platform significantly more scalable and intelligent as Simplicion Academy grows.
