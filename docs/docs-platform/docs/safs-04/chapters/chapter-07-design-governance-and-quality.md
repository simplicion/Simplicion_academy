# Chapter 7 — Design Governance & Quality

> **Normative**
>
> Every design asset, interface, component, template, interaction pattern, illustration, icon, design token, and user experience within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the governance and quality standards defined in this chapter.

---

# 7.1 Purpose

Design is a strategic organizational asset.

Without governance, products gradually become inconsistent, inaccessible, difficult to maintain, and expensive to evolve.

The purpose of this chapter is to establish a governance framework that ensures every Academy experience remains:

- consistent
- accessible
- maintainable
- scalable
- reusable
- measurable
- continuously improving

---

# 7.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Design Governance
- Ownership
- Design Reviews
- Design Documentation
- Quality Assurance
- Validation
- Accessibility Compliance
- Brand Compliance
- Version Control
- Continuous Improvement
- Metrics
- Risk Management

---

# 7.3 Governance Principles

Every Academy design SHALL follow these principles.

## Principle 1 — Consistency

Design decisions SHALL reinforce one Academy experience.

---

## Principle 2 — Accountability

Every design SHALL have an identified owner.

---

## Principle 3 — Transparency

Design decisions SHALL be documented.

---

## Principle 4 — Reusability

Reusable solutions SHALL be preferred over custom implementations.

---

## Principle 5 — Continuous Improvement

Design quality SHALL improve through feedback and measurement.

---

# 7.4 Governance Roles

Every design artifact SHALL define governance responsibilities.

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| Design Owner | Overall accountability |
| UX Designer | User experience |
| UI Designer | Visual design |
| Accessibility Reviewer | Accessibility compliance |
| Product Owner | Business alignment |
| Technical Reviewer | Implementation feasibility |
| QA Reviewer | Validation |
| Brand Reviewer | Brand consistency |
| Governance Board | Final approval |

Responsibilities SHALL remain clearly assigned.

---

# 7.6 Design Reviews

Every significant design SHALL undergo formal review.

Required reviews include:

- UX Review
- UI Review
- Accessibility Review
- Technical Review
- Brand Review
- Governance Review

Review outcomes SHALL be documented.

---

# 7.7 Quality Framework

Design quality SHALL be evaluated across multiple dimensions.

| Dimension | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Usability | Ease of use |
| Accessibility | Inclusive experience |
| Consistency | Uniform behavior |
| Performance | Efficient interaction |
| Learnability | Easy to understand |
| Discoverability | Easy to find |
| Maintainability | Easy to evolve |
| Reusability | Reusable assets |
| Brand Alignment | Consistent identity |
| Technical Feasibility | Practical implementation |

Quality SHALL be continuously monitored.

---

# 7.8 Documentation Standards

Every design SHALL include documentation.

Required sections include:

- Purpose
- Scope
- User Goals
- User Flows
- Design Decisions
- Accessibility Notes
- Responsive Behavior
- Components Used
- Design Tokens
- Version History

Documentation SHALL remain synchronized with implementation.

---

# 7.9 Version Management

Every design artifact SHALL be version controlled.

Version history SHALL record:

- author
- date
- changes
- approvals
- rationale

Design revisions SHALL remain traceable.

---

# 7.10 Design Tokens Governance

Design Tokens SHALL be centrally governed.

Changes to:

- colors
- typography
- spacing
- elevation
- shadows
- motion
- icons

SHALL require Design System approval.

Applications SHALL NOT redefine canonical tokens.

---

# 7.11 Component Governance

Reusable design components SHALL:

- follow the Design System
- remain documented
- support accessibility
- support responsiveness
- maintain compatibility

Duplicate visual components SHOULD be avoided.

---

# 7.12 Accessibility Governance

Accessibility SHALL be reviewed throughout design.

Validation SHALL verify:

- keyboard support
- screen reader compatibility
- color contrast
- focus visibility
- readable typography
- touch accessibility

Accessibility issues SHALL be resolved before release whenever practical.

---

# 7.13 Brand Governance

Every Academy interface SHALL comply with brand standards.

Brand governance SHALL include:

- logo usage
- colors
- typography
- iconography
- illustrations
- imagery
- spacing
- motion

Brand consistency SHALL remain measurable.

---

# 7.14 Prototype Validation

Interactive prototypes SHALL be validated before implementation.

Validation MAY include:

- usability testing
- stakeholder review
- accessibility review
- technical feasibility
- user feedback

Prototype findings SHALL influence implementation.

---

# 7.15 Design Testing

Design validation SHALL include:

- usability testing
- heuristic evaluation
- accessibility testing
- responsive testing
- navigation testing
- interaction testing

Testing SHALL occur before production release.

---

# 7.16 Performance Validation

Design decisions SHALL consider performance.

Evaluation SHALL include:

- rendering complexity
- animation efficiency
- interaction latency
- media optimization
- responsive performance

Design SHALL support efficient implementation.

---

# 7.17 Metrics

Design governance SHALL monitor measurable outcomes.

Representative metrics include:

| KPI | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| Usability Score | Overall usability |
| Accessibility Compliance | Accessibility conformance |
| Component Reuse | Reusable design adoption |
| Documentation Coverage | Design documentation completeness |
| Design Review Completion | Review participation |
| User Satisfaction | User feedback |
| Task Success Rate | Workflow completion |
| Design Defects | UX issues identified |

Metrics SHALL support evidence-based improvements.

---

# 7.18 Risk Management

Design governance SHALL identify and manage risks.

Examples include:

- inconsistent interfaces
- accessibility failures
- duplicate components
- poor usability
- outdated documentation
- weak navigation
- branding inconsistencies

Each risk SHALL include:

- likelihood
- impact
- owner
- mitigation plan
- review schedule

---

# 7.19 Change Management

Design changes SHALL follow formal governance.

Changes SHALL include:

- documented rationale
- stakeholder review
- impact assessment
- version update
- approval record

Unapproved changes SHALL NOT enter production.

---

# 7.20 Audit Requirements

Every governance activity SHALL create an audit record.

Audit records SHALL include:

- identifier
- reviewer
- timestamp
- decision
- affected design
- comments
- approval status

Audit history SHALL remain immutable.

---

# 7.21 Continuous Improvement

Design quality SHALL improve continuously.

Improvement sources include:

- learner feedback
- instructor feedback
- usability testing
- analytics
- accessibility reviews
- support requests
- design retrospectives

Improvements SHALL preserve consistency across Academy products.

---

# 7.22 Validation Checklist

Before publication every design SHALL satisfy:

✓ Design Tokens verified

✓ Responsive behavior validated

✓ Accessibility reviewed

✓ Navigation verified

✓ Brand compliance confirmed

✓ Documentation complete

✓ Prototype validated

✓ Usability tested

✓ Governance approved

---

# 7.23 Compliance

Design governance SHALL verify compliance with:

- SAFS-00 Academy Constitution
- SAFS-01 Academy Architecture
- SAFS-02 Data Standards
- SAFS-03 Component Standards
- SAFS-04 Design Standards
- SAFS-05 Content Standards
- SAFS-06 Governance & Lifecycle Standards

Compliance SHALL be documented.

---

# 7.24 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every Academy design SHALL have an identified owner.
2. Every design SHALL undergo formal governance review.
3. Design documentation SHALL remain synchronized with implementation.
4. Design Tokens SHALL be centrally governed.
5. Accessibility SHALL be validated before production release.
6. Brand consistency SHALL be maintained across all Academy products.
7. Significant design changes SHALL follow formal change management.
8. Design governance SHALL maintain audit records.
9. Quality metrics SHALL be monitored continuously.
10. Design improvements SHALL preserve Academy-wide consistency.

---

# 7.25 Chapter Summary

This chapter establishes the governance and quality framework for every design artifact within Simplicion Academy.

It defines:

- governance principles
- ownership
- lifecycle management
- review processes
- quality assurance
- documentation
- version management
- Design Token governance
- component governance
- accessibility governance
- brand governance
- prototype validation
- design testing
- performance validation
- metrics
- risk management
- change management
- auditing
- continuous improvement
- compliance

Together, these standards ensure that every Academy design remains consistent, accessible, reusable, measurable, and aligned with the Academy Design System throughout its lifecycle.

The final chapter, **Chapter 8 — Design Reference & Appendices**, consolidates the canonical design token catalog, visual style guide, component references, layout templates, iconography library, terminology, validation checklists, implementation guidance, and cross-reference matrices supporting the complete Design Standards.
