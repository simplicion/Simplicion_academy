# Chapter 7 — Content Governance & Quality

> **Normative**
>
> Every educational resource, knowledge asset, assessment, documentation, media asset, AI-generated content, operational document, and published resource within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the governance and quality standards defined in this chapter.

---

# 7.1 Purpose

Governance ensures that Academy content remains trustworthy, consistent, accurate, and aligned with organizational objectives throughout its lifecycle.

The purpose of this chapter is to establish one unified governance framework that enables every content asset to be:

- accountable
- auditable
- maintainable
- measurable
- compliant
- continuously improving

Governance SHALL extend across the complete content lifecycle.

---

# 7.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Governance Principles
- Content Ownership
- Editorial Governance
- Quality Management
- Review Processes
- Compliance
- Risk Management
- Audit
- Change Management
- Metrics
- Continuous Improvement
- Organizational Accountability

---

# 7.3 Governance Principles

Every content asset SHALL follow these governance principles.

## Principle 1 — Accountability

Every content asset SHALL have a clearly assigned owner.

---

## Principle 2 — Accuracy

Published content SHALL remain factually correct.

---

## Principle 3 — Consistency

Content SHALL follow Academy standards for terminology, formatting, and structure.

---

## Principle 4 — Transparency

Content decisions SHALL be documented.

---

## Principle 5 — Continuous Improvement

Content SHALL evolve through measurable improvements.

---

# 7.4 Governance Roles

Every content asset SHALL define governance responsibilities.

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| Content Owner | Overall accountability |
| Author | Content creation |
| Editor | Editorial quality |
| Subject Matter Expert | Technical accuracy |
| Accessibility Reviewer | Accessibility validation |
| Quality Reviewer | Quality assurance |
| Product Owner | Business alignment |
| Governance Board | Final approval |

Responsibilities SHALL remain current throughout the content lifecycle.

---

# 7.6 Editorial Governance

Editorial governance SHALL verify:

- grammar
- readability
- consistency
- terminology
- formatting
- references

Editorial standards SHALL remain consistent across all Academy content.

---

# 7.7 Educational Governance

Educational governance SHALL verify:

- learning objectives
- instructional quality
- competency alignment
- curriculum consistency
- assessment alignment

Educational governance SHALL prioritize learner success.

---

# 7.8 AI Content Governance

AI-generated content SHALL be governed.

Governance SHALL verify:

- factual accuracy
- authoritative sources
- transparency
- safety
- policy compliance
- human review where retained as Academy knowledge

AI SHALL support—not replace—governed Academy knowledge.

---

# 7.9 Accessibility Governance

Accessibility governance SHALL evaluate:

- semantic structure
- captions
- transcripts
- alternative text
- keyboard accessibility
- readable language
- inclusive design

Accessibility SHALL remain measurable.

---

# 7.10 Metadata Governance

Metadata SHALL remain complete and accurate.

Governance SHALL validate:

- identifiers
- ownership
- keywords
- taxonomy
- relationships
- lifecycle state
- version information

Metadata SHALL comply with SAFS-02.

---

# 7.11 Version Governance

Every published asset SHALL maintain version history.

Version governance SHALL record:

- author
- reviewer
- approver
- publication date
- revision summary
- superseded versions

Version history SHALL remain auditable.

---

# 7.12 Quality Framework

Content quality SHALL be evaluated across multiple dimensions.

| Dimension | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Accuracy | Correct information |
| Completeness | Required information included |
| Clarity | Easy to understand |
| Consistency | Uniform presentation |
| Accessibility | Inclusive learning |
| Relevance | Current usefulness |
| Maintainability | Easy updates |
| Discoverability | Easy retrieval |
| Reusability | Modular content |
| Compliance | Standards adherence |

Quality SHALL be reviewed periodically.

---

# 7.13 Compliance Management

Content SHALL comply with:

- Academy Constitution
- Architecture Standards
- Data Standards
- Component Standards
- Design Standards
- Content Standards
- Governance Standards

Compliance SHALL be documented.

---

# 7.14 Risk Management

Content governance SHALL identify and manage risks.

Examples include:

- inaccurate information
- outdated resources
- inconsistent terminology
- broken references
- accessibility failures
- duplicate content
- licensing violations
- governance gaps

Every identified risk SHALL define:

- likelihood
- impact
- mitigation strategy
- owner
- review schedule

---

# 7.15 Change Management

Content changes SHALL follow a governed process.

Every significant change SHALL include:

- change request
- rationale
- impact assessment
- review
- approval
- version update

Unauthorized changes SHALL NOT be published.

---

# 7.16 Audit Requirements

Governance activities SHALL generate audit records.

Audit records SHALL include:

- content identifier
- activity
- reviewer
- timestamp
- approval decision
- comments
- version

Audit history SHALL remain immutable.

---

Representative metrics include:

| KPI | Description |

| Accessibility Compliance | Accessibility conformance |
| Review Completion | Review efficiency |
| Content Freshness | Current information |
| Publication Success | Successful releases |
| Metadata Completeness | Metadata quality |
| Reuse Rate | Content reuse |

---

# 7.18 Policy Management

Governance policies SHALL define:

- publishing rules
- editorial standards
- review requirements
- approval authority
- retention policies
- archival requirements

Policies SHALL remain version controlled.

---

# 7.19 Continuous Improvement

Content SHALL improve continuously.

Improvement sources include:

- learner feedback
- instructor feedback
- analytics
- audits
- accessibility reviews
- quality assessments
- editorial findings

Improvements SHALL preserve canonical identifiers wherever practical.

---

# 7.20 Business Continuity

Governance SHALL support continuity.

Continuity SHALL include:

- repository backup
- documentation preservation
- version recovery
- archive restoration
- operational resilience

Recovery procedures SHALL be documented.

---

# 7.21 Validation Checklist

Before publication every governed content asset SHALL satisfy:

✓ Ownership assigned

✓ Editorial review completed

✓ Subject review completed

✓ Accessibility validated

✓ Metadata complete

✓ Version assigned

✓ Audit record created

✓ Governance approved

---

# 7.24 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every content asset SHALL identify a Content Owner.
2. Editorial, subject matter, and accessibility reviews SHALL precede publication.
3. AI-generated content retained as Academy knowledge SHALL undergo governance review.
4. Metadata SHALL remain complete and compliant with SAFS-02.
5. Every published asset SHALL maintain version history.
6. Governance activities SHALL generate immutable audit records.
7. Content quality SHALL be measured using defined KPIs.
8. Significant content changes SHALL follow formal change management.
9. Governance policies SHALL remain version controlled.
10. Content governance SHALL remain technology independent.
