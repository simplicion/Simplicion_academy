# Chapter 5 — Media & Digital Asset Standards

> **Normative**
>
> Every image, illustration, video, audio recording, animation, document, presentation, downloadable resource, diagram, interactive asset, and multimedia object within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the Media & Digital Asset Standards defined in this chapter.

---

# 5.1 Purpose

Media enriches learning by making information easier to understand, remember, and apply.

The purpose of this chapter is to establish one unified standard for managing every digital asset throughout its lifecycle while ensuring:

- quality
- accessibility
- consistency
- discoverability
- security
- maintainability
- reusability

Media SHALL be treated as a governed organizational asset.

---

# 5.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Media Architecture
- Asset Classification
- Images
- Videos
- Audio
- Documents
- Interactive Media
- Diagrams
- Accessibility
- Metadata
- Licensing
- Versioning
- Storage
- Governance

---

# 5.3 Media Architecture

Every digital asset SHALL belong to the canonical architecture.

```text
Academy
   │
   ▼
Repository
   │
   ▼
Collection
   │
   ▼
Asset Category
   │
   ▼
Digital Asset
   │
   ▼
Version
   │
   ▼
Distribution
   │
   ▼
Archive
```

Every asset SHALL have a single authoritative source.

---

# 5.4 Asset Categories

The Academy SHALL support standardized asset categories.

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Images | PNG, JPG, SVG |
| Illustrations | Vector artwork |
| Icons | UI icons |
| Videos | Lectures, tutorials |
| Audio | Podcasts, narration |
| Documents | PDF, DOCX |
| Presentations | PPTX |
| Diagrams | Flowcharts, architecture |
| Interactive Assets | Simulations |
| Templates | Downloadable resources |

Categories SHALL remain centrally governed.

---

# 5.5 Images

Images SHALL:

- support learning
- maintain high quality
- remain optimized
- include descriptive metadata
- include alternative text

Images SHALL reinforce educational objectives rather than serve decorative purposes alone.

---

# 5.6 Illustrations

Illustrations SHALL:

- simplify complex concepts
- remain visually consistent
- support Academy branding
- remain reusable
- maintain scalability

Illustrations SHOULD follow the Academy Design System.

---

# 5.7 Video Standards

Educational videos SHALL include:

- title
- description
- transcript
- captions
- duration
- author
- version

Videos SHOULD be divided into logical learning segments.

Large videos SHOULD be chapterized.

---

# 5.8 Audio Standards

Audio assets SHALL include:

- narration
- podcasts
- pronunciation guides
- interviews
- sound effects (where appropriate)

Audio SHALL maintain clear speech and consistent volume.

Transcripts SHALL accompany educational audio.

---

# 5.9 Documents

Documents MAY include:

- study guides
- manuals
- reference material
- worksheets
- templates
- policy documents

Documents SHALL remain searchable.

---

# 5.10 Diagrams

Diagrams SHALL communicate information clearly.

Supported diagram categories include:

- Process Flow
- Architecture
- Entity Relationship
- Workflow
- Timeline
- Mind Map
- Organizational Structure
- Decision Tree

Diagrams SHALL remain readable across supported devices.

---

# 5.11 Interactive Media

Interactive assets MAY include:

- simulations
- coding sandboxes
- quizzes
- calculators
- drag-and-drop exercises
- virtual laboratories

Interactive assets SHALL support measurable learning outcomes.

---

# 5.12 Accessibility

Every media asset SHALL support accessibility.

Requirements include:

- alternative text
- captions
- transcripts
- keyboard accessibility
- readable visuals
- sufficient color contrast
- descriptive labels

Accessibility SHALL comply with SAFS-04.

---

# 5.13 Media Metadata

Every asset SHALL include canonical metadata.

Required metadata includes:

- identifier
- title
- description
- owner
- creator
- version
- language
- keywords
- accessibility status
- copyright status
- lifecycle state

Metadata SHALL comply with SAFS-02.

---

# 5.14 File Standards

Digital assets SHALL use supported formats.

Examples include:

| Asset | Preferred Formats |
|---------|------------------|
| Images | PNG, SVG, JPG |
| Video | MP4 |
| Audio | MP3, WAV |
| Documents | PDF |
| Presentations | PPTX |
| Diagrams | SVG, PNG |

Additional formats MAY be supported where justified.

---

# 5.15 Naming Conventions

Digital assets SHALL use standardized names.

Example:

```text
academy-course-introduction-v1.mp4

module-02-network-diagram.svg

lesson-05-example-01.png

assessment-rubric-v2.pdf
```

Names SHALL remain descriptive and unique.

---

# 5.16 Version Management

Every media asset SHALL maintain version history.

Version records SHALL include:

- author
- revision date
- changes
- approval
- replacement asset

Previous versions SHALL remain auditable.

---

# 5.17 Storage & Repository

Media SHALL be stored within approved repositories.

Repositories SHALL support:

- indexing
- search
- access control
- backup
- recovery
- versioning

Storage SHALL remain technology independent.

---

# 5.18 Distribution

Media MAY be distributed through:

- Academy Platform
- Mobile Applications
- LMS
- APIs
- Downloads
- Offline Packages

Distribution SHALL preserve asset integrity.

---

# 5.19 Licensing & Copyright

Every asset SHALL define usage rights.

Licensing SHALL identify:

- owner
- copyright holder
- permitted use
- attribution requirements
- expiration (if applicable)

Unauthorized media SHALL NOT be published.

---

# 5.20 Security

Protected media SHALL support:

- access control
- secure delivery
- watermarking (where appropriate)
- audit logging
- integrity verification

Sensitive assets SHALL receive additional protection.

---

# 5.21 AI-Generated Media

AI MAY assist media creation.

Examples include:

- illustrations
- voice narration
- animations
- diagrams
- subtitles
- summaries

AI-generated assets SHALL:

- be reviewed
- identify ownership
- meet Academy quality standards

---

# 5.22 Quality Assurance

Media quality SHALL evaluate:

- resolution
- readability
- accessibility
- educational value
- technical quality
- branding
- optimization

Quality SHALL be reviewed before publication.

---

# 5.23 Validation

Before publication every media asset SHALL satisfy:

✓ Metadata complete

✓ Accessibility verified

✓ Copyright reviewed

✓ File format approved

✓ Quality validated

✓ Naming convention verified

✓ Repository registration completed

✓ Governance approved

---

# 5.24 Governance

Media assets SHALL undergo:

- Editorial Review
- Technical Review
- Accessibility Review
- Copyright Review
- Brand Review
- Governance Approval

Review outcomes SHALL be documented.

---

# 5.25 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every digital asset SHALL have a canonical repository location.
2. Every asset SHALL include standardized metadata.
3. Educational videos SHALL provide captions and transcripts.
4. Every media asset SHALL satisfy accessibility requirements.
5. Naming conventions SHALL remain consistent.
6. Media SHALL maintain complete version history.
7. Copyright and licensing SHALL be documented before publication.
8. AI-generated media SHALL undergo human review.
9. Media assets SHALL undergo governance approval before publication.
10. Media standards SHALL remain technology independent.

---
