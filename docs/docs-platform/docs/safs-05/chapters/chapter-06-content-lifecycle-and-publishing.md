# Chapter 6 — Content Operations & Publishing

> **Normative**
>
> Every content creation, editorial activity, publishing process, localization effort, automation workflow, release, archival process, and operational activity within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the standards defined in this chapter.

---

# 6.1 Purpose

Content Operations transforms knowledge into published learning experiences through structured, repeatable, and governed processes.

The purpose of this chapter is to establish one standardized operational framework that ensures Academy content is:

- accurate
- consistent
- traceable
- scalable
- reusable
- continuously maintained

Operations SHALL prioritize reliability over manual processes wherever practical.

---

# 6.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Content Authoring
- Editorial Workflows
- Review Processes
- Publishing
- Localization
- Translation
- Release Management
- Scheduling
- Automation
- Archiving
- Operational Metrics
- Continuous Improvement

---

# 6.3 Content Operations Architecture

Content SHALL move through a standardized operational workflow.

```text
Planning
   │
   ▼
Authoring
   │
   ▼
Editorial Review
   │
   ▼
Subject Review
   │
   ▼
Accessibility Review
   │
   ▼
Quality Assurance
   │
   ▼
Approval
   │
   ▼
Publishing
   │
   ▼
Distribution
   │
   ▼
Monitoring
   │
   ▼
Maintenance
   │
   ▼
Archive
```

Every published asset SHALL follow this lifecycle.

---

# 6.4 Content Planning

Planning SHALL define:

- objectives
- audience
- learning outcomes
- dependencies
- delivery channels
- publication schedule
- owners

Planning SHALL occur before authoring begins.

---

# 6.5 Content Authoring

Authors SHALL create content using Academy standards.

Authoring SHALL include:

- canonical structure
- approved terminology
- reusable content blocks
- metadata
- accessibility considerations

Authors SHALL use approved templates wherever available.

---

# 6.6 Editorial Workflow

Editorial review SHALL evaluate:

- grammar
- clarity
- consistency
- formatting
- terminology
- readability
- references

Editorial approval SHALL precede publication.

---

# 6.7 Subject Matter Review

Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) SHALL verify:

- factual accuracy
- technical correctness
- educational validity
- completeness
- relevance

SME approval SHALL be documented.

---

# 6.8 Accessibility Review

Accessibility review SHALL verify:

- semantic structure
- headings
- captions
- transcripts
- alternative text
- readable language
- keyboard accessibility (where applicable)

Accessibility SHALL be integrated before publication.

---

# 6.9 Quality Assurance

Content Quality Assurance SHALL verify:

- metadata completeness
- broken links
- media integrity
- formatting
- navigation
- assessment mappings
- competency mappings

Quality validation SHALL be repeatable.

---

# 6.10 Publishing Workflow

Publishing SHALL follow an approved workflow.

```text
Approved
   │
   ▼
Publish
   │
   ▼
 Index
   │
   ▼
Distribute
   │
   ▼
 Verify
   │
   ▼
Monitor
```

Publishing SHALL generate an auditable record.

---

# 6.11 Publishing Channels

Content MAY be published through:

- Academy Platform
- Learning Management Systems
- Mobile Applications
- APIs
- Documentation Portals
- Download Packages
- Offline Learning Systems

Published content SHALL remain synchronized across supported channels.

---

# 6.12 Localization

Localization SHALL adapt content appropriately for different locales.

Localization MAY include:

- language
- formatting
- cultural references
- examples
- measurements
- regional conventions

Localized content SHALL preserve educational intent.

---

# 6.13 Translation

Translations SHALL maintain:

- accuracy
- terminology
- meaning
- accessibility
- metadata
- relationships

Canonical Academy terminology SHALL remain consistent across languages.

---

# 6.14 Scheduling

Publishing schedules SHALL define:

- publication date
- revision schedule
- review cycle
- retirement date (if applicable)

Schedules SHALL be centrally managed.

---

# 6.15 Release Management

Every content release SHALL include:

- release identifier
- version
- release notes
- affected resources
- approvals
- rollback plan (where applicable)

Releases SHALL remain traceable.

---

# 6.16 Automation

Automation MAY support:

- publishing
- indexing
- notifications
- metadata validation
- link checking
- scheduled reviews
- archive management

Automation SHALL complement—not replace—governance.

---

# 6.17 Notifications

Operational notifications MAY include:

- publication completed
- review required
- revision due
- approval requested
- retirement scheduled
- validation failures

Notifications SHALL support operational efficiency.

---

# 6.18 Content Maintenance

Published content SHALL be maintained throughout its lifecycle.

Maintenance activities include:

- correcting inaccuracies
- updating references
- replacing obsolete material
- improving accessibility
- enhancing examples
- updating metadata

Maintenance SHALL preserve canonical identifiers where practical.

---

# 6.19 Archiving

Archived content SHALL:

- remain identifiable
- remain versioned
- preserve historical references
- prevent unintended publication

Archived resources SHALL remain recoverable.

---

# 6.20 Operational Analytics

Content Operations SHALL monitor measurable indicators.

Representative metrics include:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Publication Time | Draft to publication |
| Review Completion | Review efficiency |
| Editorial Defects | Quality findings |
| Accessibility Compliance | Accessibility status |
| Revision Frequency | Update cadence |
| Publication Success Rate | Successful releases |
| Automation Coverage | Automated operations |
| Archive Accuracy | Proper archival management |

Metrics SHALL support continuous operational improvement.

---

# 6.21 Audit Logging

Operational activities SHALL generate audit records.

Audit entries SHALL include:

- activity
- timestamp
- actor
- affected content
- approval status
- version

Audit history SHALL remain immutable.

---

# 6.22 Business Continuity

Content Operations SHALL support continuity.

Continuity SHALL include:

- repository backups
- recovery procedures
- publishing recovery
- archive restoration
- operational redundancy

Recovery procedures SHALL be documented.

---

# 6.23 Validation

Before publication every content release SHALL satisfy:

✓ Editorial review completed

✓ SME approval completed

✓ Accessibility verified

✓ Metadata validated

✓ Quality assurance completed

✓ Release notes prepared

✓ Repository updated

✓ Governance approved

---

# 6.24 Governance

Content Operations SHALL undergo governance.

Governance SHALL include:

- Editorial Governance
- Educational Governance
- Accessibility Governance
- Operational Governance
- Release Governance

Governance decisions SHALL be documented.

---

# 6.25 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every content asset SHALL follow the canonical operational workflow.
2. Editorial review SHALL precede publication.
3. Subject Matter Experts SHALL validate educational accuracy.
4. Accessibility SHALL be reviewed before publication.
5. Every content release SHALL include version information and release notes.
6. Localization and translation SHALL preserve educational meaning.
7. Operational automation SHALL remain governed and auditable.
8. Published content SHALL be maintained throughout its lifecycle.
9. Operational activities SHALL generate immutable audit records.
10. Content Operations SHALL remain technology independent.
