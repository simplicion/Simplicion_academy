# Chapter 4 — AI & Knowledge Content

> **Normative**
>
> Every AI-ready resource, Knowledge Base, prompt, semantic document, embedding source, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) asset, AI-generated response, and machine-readable educational resource within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the standards defined in this chapter.

---

# 4.1 Purpose

Artificial Intelligence depends on structured, trustworthy, and governed knowledge.

The purpose of this chapter is to ensure Academy knowledge can be consumed effectively by:

- AI Assistants
- Intelligent Tutors
- Recommendation Engines
- Search Systems
- Knowledge Graphs
- Analytics Platforms
- Automation Systems
- Future AI technologies

AI SHALL consume authoritative Academy knowledge rather than unmanaged content.

---

# 4.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Knowledge Bases
- AI-Ready Content
- Semantic Structure
- Prompt Libraries
- Embeddings
- Retrieval
- AI Responses
- Knowledge Relationships
- AI Metadata
- Knowledge Governance
- AI Safety
- Continuous Improvement

---

# 4.3 AI Knowledge Philosophy

The Academy adopts the following philosophy.

> **Content created for humans SHALL also be understandable by machines.**

Knowledge SHALL be:

- structured
- modular
- traceable
- reusable
- explainable
- versioned
- governed

AI SHALL enhance learning without replacing authoritative Academy knowledge.

---

Canonical AI Knowledge Architecture:

```text
Academy Knowledge
       │
       ▼

       │
       ▼
    Metadata
       │
       ▼
 Knowledge Base
       │
       ▼
   Embeddings
       │
       ▼
  Vector Index
       │
       ▼
   Retrieval
       │
       ▼
  AI Reasoning
       │
       ▼
   AI Response
```

Every AI interaction SHALL originate from governed Academy knowledge.

---

# 4.5 Knowledge Bases

The Academy SHALL maintain centralized Knowledge Bases.

Knowledge Base categories include:

| Knowledge Base | Purpose |
|----------------|---------|
| Curriculum | Learning content |
| Competencies | Skills framework |
| Assessments | Question repositories |
| Documentation | Product knowledge |
| Policies | Governance |
| Repository | Digital resources |
| AI Prompts | Prompt templates |
| Glossary | Canonical terminology |

Knowledge Bases SHALL remain version controlled.

---

# 4.6 AI-Ready Content

AI-ready content SHALL include:

- semantic headings
- structured sections
- canonical terminology
- reusable knowledge units
- metadata
- explicit relationships

Content SHALL avoid ambiguous structure wherever practical.

---

# 4.7 Knowledge Units

Knowledge SHALL be modular.

Examples include:

- Definitions
- Concepts
- Procedures
- Examples
- Best Practices
- FAQs
- Case Studies
- Troubleshooting Guides
- Policies
- References

Knowledge Units SHALL remain independently reusable.

---

# 4.8 Semantic Structure

Semantic organization SHALL improve AI understanding.

Recommended hierarchy:

```text
Domain
  │
  ▼
Subject
  │
  ▼
Concept
  │
  ▼
Knowledge Unit
  │
  ▼
Supporting Resources
```

Semantic structure SHALL remain consistent throughout the Academy.

---

# 4.9 Prompt Library

Prompt Libraries SHALL be centrally governed.

Prompt categories MAY include:

- Tutoring
- Assessment
- Coaching
- Recommendation
- Content Generation
- Summarization
- Translation
- Search
- Analytics
- Administration

Prompts SHALL include documentation and version history.

---

# 4.10 Prompt Standards

Every prompt SHALL define:

- identifier
- purpose
- expected inputs
- expected outputs
- safety constraints
- owner
- version
- approval status

Prompt behavior SHALL remain predictable.

---

# 4.11 Embeddings

Embeddings SHALL represent canonical Academy knowledge.

Embedding sources MAY include:

- Learning Units
- Documentation
- Policies
- Glossaries
- Assessments
- Competencies
- Reference Materials

Embeddings SHALL preserve relationships between knowledge objects.

---

# 4.12 Retrieval Standards

AI retrieval SHALL prioritize:

- authoritative knowledge
- relevance
- semantic similarity
- freshness
- governance status
- user context

Retrieval SHALL explain information sources whenever practical.

---

# 4.13 Knowledge Relationships

Knowledge SHALL define explicit relationships.

Examples include:

- prerequisite
- parent concept
- child concept
- related topic
- supporting evidence
- competency mapping
- assessment mapping

Relationships SHALL use canonical identifiers.

---

# 4.14 AI Responses

AI-generated responses SHALL:

- remain accurate
- reference authoritative knowledge
- distinguish generated content
- communicate uncertainty where appropriate
- avoid unsupported conclusions

Responses SHALL prioritize learner understanding.

---

# 4.15 Explainability

AI systems SHOULD explain:

- why information was selected
- supporting knowledge sources
- reasoning process (where appropriate)
- confidence indicators

Explainability SHALL improve user trust.

---

# 4.16 AI Safety

AI-generated content SHALL comply with Academy governance.

Safety requirements include:

- harmful content prevention
- misinformation mitigation
- privacy protection
- policy compliance
- prompt protection
- abuse monitoring

Safety SHALL be continuously evaluated.

---

# 4.17 AI Metadata

Every AI Knowledge Unit SHALL include metadata.

Required metadata includes:

- identifier
- knowledge category
- owner
- version
- lifecycle
- language
- source
- confidence level (where applicable)
- embedding status

Metadata SHALL comply with SAFS-02.

---

# 4.18 Knowledge Versioning

Knowledge SHALL remain version controlled.

Version history SHALL record:

- revisions
- approvals
- publication dates
- retirement
- replacement resources

Historical versions SHALL remain auditable.

---

# 4.19 AI Personalization

AI MAY personalize learning experiences.

Personalization MAY consider:

- learner role
- competencies
- completed learning
- language
- accessibility preferences
- learning objectives

Personalization SHALL preserve authoritative content.

---

# 4.20 AI Analytics

Analytics SHALL measure AI effectiveness.

Representative metrics include:

- retrieval accuracy
- response relevance
- user satisfaction
- knowledge coverage
- prompt performance
- recommendation acceptance
- unresolved questions

Metrics SHALL support continuous improvement.

---

# 4.21 AI Content Validation

Before publication every AI knowledge asset SHALL satisfy:

✓ Metadata complete

✓ Knowledge reviewed

✓ Prompt validated

✓ Retrieval verified

✓ Relationships mapped

✓ Accessibility reviewed

✓ Governance approved

---

# 4.22 AI Governance

AI Knowledge SHALL undergo governance.

Required reviews include:

- Subject Matter Review
- AI Review
- Editorial Review
- Accessibility Review
- Governance Approval

AI-generated content SHALL remain traceable.

---

# 4.23 Knowledge Maintenance

Knowledge SHALL be reviewed periodically.

Maintenance activities include:

- updating obsolete information
- improving semantic structure
- refining prompts
- validating embeddings
- correcting inaccuracies
- expanding knowledge coverage

Maintenance SHALL preserve canonical identifiers.

---

# 4.24 Future AI Compatibility

Academy knowledge SHALL remain compatible with future AI systems.

Knowledge SHOULD remain:

- model independent
- vendor independent
- technology independent
- semantically structured
- interoperable

Future AI capabilities SHALL build upon the same canonical knowledge foundation.

---

# 4.25 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every AI Knowledge Base SHALL use canonical Academy knowledge.
2. AI-ready content SHALL include structured semantic organization.
3. Prompt Libraries SHALL remain centrally governed.
4. AI retrieval SHALL prioritize authoritative and governed knowledge.
5. AI responses SHALL distinguish generated content from Academy-authored content.
6. AI systems SHALL provide explainability where appropriate.
7. AI Knowledge SHALL include canonical metadata.
8. AI-generated content SHALL undergo governance before publication where retained as Academy knowledge.
9. Knowledge SHALL remain version controlled and traceable.
10. AI knowledge standards SHALL remain technology and vendor independent.

---
