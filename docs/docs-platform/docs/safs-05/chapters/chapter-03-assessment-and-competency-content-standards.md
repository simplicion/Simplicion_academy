# Chapter 3 — Assessment & Evaluation Content

> **Normative**
>
> Every assessment, examination, quiz, assignment, project, practical evaluation, certification, rubric, grading model, and learner feedback mechanism within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the Assessment & Evaluation Content standards defined in this chapter.

---

# 3.1 Purpose

Assessment measures learning progress, validates competency, and provides meaningful feedback that supports continuous improvement.

Assessment SHALL evaluate whether learners have achieved the intended learning outcomes rather than merely measuring content exposure.

Every assessment SHALL be:

- valid
- reliable
- fair
- transparent
- measurable
- accessible
- continuously improvable

---

# 3.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Assessment Design
- Evaluation Models
- Question Banks
- Practical Assessments
- Projects
- Rubrics
- Competency Evaluation
- Grading
- Learner Feedback
- Certification
- Assessment Analytics
- Governance

---

# 3.3 Assessment Philosophy

Assessment SHALL support learning rather than simply assigning scores.

Assessment SHOULD:

- reinforce understanding
- encourage reflection
- identify knowledge gaps
- measure competencies
- motivate continuous improvement

Evaluation SHALL remain learner-centered.

---

Every assessment SHALL follow the canonical Academy hierarchy.

      │

      │
      ▼

      │
      ▼

Assessments SHALL align directly with Learning Units.

---

# 3.5 Assessment Types

The Academy supports multiple assessment types.

| Assessment Type | Purpose |
|-----------------|---------|
| Diagnostic | Determine initial knowledge |
| Formative | Monitor progress |
| Practice | Reinforce learning |
| Summative | Measure achievement |
| Practical | Evaluate applied skills |
| Project | Demonstrate competency |
| Peer Review | Collaborative evaluation |
| Self Assessment | Reflection |
| Certification | Validate mastery |

Each assessment SHALL clearly identify its purpose.

---

# 3.6 Learning Outcome Alignment

Every assessment SHALL map to one or more learning objectives.

Assessment SHALL verify:

- knowledge
- understanding
- application
- analysis
- evaluation
- creation

Learning objectives SHALL be measurable.

---

# 3.7 Competency Mapping

Assessments SHALL evaluate defined competencies.

Examples include:

- technical competency
- analytical thinking
- communication
- collaboration
- leadership
- problem solving
- professional ethics

Competencies SHALL use canonical identifiers defined in SAFS-02.

---

# 3.8 Question Banks

Question Banks SHALL be centrally managed.

Question categories MAY include:

- Multiple Choice
- Multiple Select
- True/False
- Matching
- Fill in the Blank
- Short Answer
- Long Answer
- Coding Challenge
- Case Study
- Simulation

Questions SHALL remain reusable.

---

# 3.9 Question Design

Every question SHALL include:

- identifier
- learning objective
- competency mapping
- difficulty
- expected answer
- explanation
- version
- metadata

Questions SHALL remain independently reusable.

---

# 3.10 Difficulty Levels

Assessment items SHALL identify difficulty.

Canonical levels include:

| Level | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Beginner | Fundamental understanding |
| Intermediate | Applied understanding |
| Advanced | Complex application |
| Expert | Professional mastery |

Difficulty SHALL align with curriculum expectations.

---

# 3.11 Practical Assessments

Practical assessments SHALL evaluate real-world application.

Examples include:

- coding exercises
- design projects
- research assignments
- presentations
- simulations
- laboratory activities
- collaborative projects

Practical assessments SHALL define measurable evaluation criteria.

---

# 3.12 Projects

Projects SHALL evaluate integrated competencies.

Every project SHALL define:

- objectives
- scope
- deliverables
- milestones
- evaluation criteria
- completion requirements

Projects SHALL encourage authentic problem solving.

---

# 3.13 Rubrics

Rubrics SHALL standardize evaluation.

Every rubric SHOULD define:

- evaluation criteria
- performance levels
- scoring guidance
- competency alignment
- reviewer notes

Rubrics SHALL improve consistency and transparency.

---

# 3.14 Grading Standards

Grading SHALL be fair and transparent.

Grading MAY include:

- percentage scores
- competency achievement
- pass/fail
- proficiency levels
- weighted evaluation

Grading methods SHALL be documented.

---

# 3.15 Feedback

Assessment SHALL provide meaningful feedback.

Feedback SHOULD include:

- strengths
- improvement opportunities
- references
- recommended next steps
- related learning resources

Feedback SHALL encourage continued learning.

---

# 3.16 Adaptive Assessment

Adaptive assessment MAY personalize evaluation.

Adaptation MAY consider:

- learner progress
- previous performance
- competency mastery
- difficulty progression

Adaptation SHALL preserve fairness.

---

# 3.17 AI-Assisted Evaluation

AI MAY assist evaluation.

Supported capabilities include:

- automated feedback
- answer analysis
- coding evaluation
- plagiarism detection
- recommendation generation

Final certification decisions SHALL remain governed by Academy policies.

---

# 3.18 Certification

Certification SHALL verify competency achievement.

Certification SHALL define:

- required competencies
- completion requirements
- assessment thresholds
- validity period (where applicable)
- issuing authority

Certificates SHALL use canonical metadata.

---

# 3.19 Assessment Integrity

Assessment integrity SHALL be protected.

Integrity measures MAY include:

- identity verification
- time controls
- plagiarism detection
- randomized questions
- secure delivery
- audit logging

Integrity mechanisms SHALL balance security with learner experience.

---

# 3.20 Accessibility

Assessments SHALL remain accessible.

Requirements include:

- keyboard accessibility
- screen reader support
- accessible media
- sufficient timing
- readable language
- alternative formats where appropriate

Accessibility SHALL not compromise assessment validity.

---

# 3.21 Assessment Metadata

Every assessment SHALL include metadata.

Required metadata includes:

- identifier
- assessment type
- learning objectives
- competencies
- difficulty
- estimated duration
- passing criteria
- version
- owner

Metadata SHALL comply with SAFS-02.

---

# 3.22 Assessment Analytics

Assessment analytics SHALL support continuous improvement.

Representative metrics include:

- completion rate
- pass rate
- average score
- competency attainment
- question difficulty
- discrimination index
- learner satisfaction

Analytics SHALL inform curriculum improvements.

---

# 3.23 Documentation

Assessment documentation SHALL include:

- purpose
- objectives
- competencies
- grading model
- rubrics
- scoring guidance
- revision history

Documentation SHALL remain synchronized with assessment content.

---

# 3.24 Validation

Before publication every assessment SHALL satisfy:

✓ Learning objectives verified

✓ Competencies mapped

✓ Questions reviewed

✓ Rubrics validated

✓ Accessibility reviewed

✓ Metadata complete

✓ Editorial approval completed

✓ Subject Matter Expert approval completed

✓ Governance approved

---

# 3.25 Governance

Assessments SHALL undergo:

- Editorial Review
- Subject Matter Review
- Assessment Design Review
- Accessibility Review
- Quality Review
- Governance Approval

Review findings SHALL be documented.

---

# 3.26 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every assessment SHALL align with defined learning objectives.
2. Assessment items SHALL map to canonical competencies.
3. Question Banks SHALL support reusable assessment content.
4. Practical assessments SHALL define measurable evaluation criteria.
5. Rubrics SHALL standardize evaluation.
6. Assessment feedback SHALL support continued learning.
7. AI-assisted evaluation SHALL remain transparent and governed.
8. Accessibility SHALL be incorporated into every assessment.
9. Assessments SHALL undergo governance review before publication.
10. Assessment standards SHALL remain technology independent.

---
