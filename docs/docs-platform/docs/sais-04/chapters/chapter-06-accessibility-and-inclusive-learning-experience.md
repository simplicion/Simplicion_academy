# Chapter 6 — Accessibility & Inclusive Learning Experience Standards

> **Normative**
>
> Every digital experience delivered by Simplicion Academy SHALL be accessible, inclusive, equitable, and usable by the widest possible range of learners regardless of ability, language, device, age, connectivity, or learning preference.

---

# 6.1 Purpose

Accessibility is not a feature—it is a fundamental quality of every learning experience.

The Academy SHALL ensure that every learner has equal opportunity to:

- discover learning
- access content
- participate in activities
- complete assessments
- collaborate with others
- earn certifications
- continue lifelong learning

Accessibility SHALL be integrated from the beginning of every design and implementation process.

---

# 6.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Universal Design
- Inclusive Learning
- Accessibility Compliance
- Assistive Technologies
- Keyboard Navigation
- Screen Readers
- Visual Accessibility
- Hearing Accessibility
- Cognitive Accessibility
- Motor Accessibility
- Multilingual Learning
- Localization
- Offline Learning
- Low-Bandwidth Experiences
- Accessibility Governance

---

# 6.3 Accessibility Philosophy

The Academy adopts the following philosophy.

> **Every learner deserves equal access to knowledge without unnecessary barriers.**

Accessibility SHALL be:

- proactive
- inclusive
- learner-centered
- standards-based
- continuously improved

Accessibility SHALL never be treated as an optional enhancement.

---

# 6.4 Universal Design Principles

Every learning experience SHALL follow Universal Design principles.

The Academy adopts the following framework.

```text
Equitable Use
     │
     ▼
Flexible Use
     │
     ▼
Simple & Intuitive
     │
     ▼
Perceivable Information
     │
     ▼
Tolerance for Error
     │
     ▼
Low Physical Effort
     │
     ▼
Appropriate Size & Space
```

These principles SHALL guide all learner-facing experiences.

---

# 6.5 Accessibility Compliance

Every Academy product SHALL comply with recognized accessibility standards.

Compliance SHALL include:

- WCAG alignment
- semantic markup
- keyboard accessibility
- assistive technology compatibility
- accessible forms
- accessible media
- responsive layouts

Accessibility compliance SHALL be validated before release.

---

# 6.6 Keyboard Accessibility

Every function SHALL be usable without a mouse.

Keyboard support SHALL include:

- logical tab order
- visible focus indicators
- keyboard shortcuts (where appropriate)
- skip navigation
- modal navigation
- accessible menus

No essential feature SHALL require pointer input.

---

# 6.7 Screen Reader Compatibility

Every learner SHALL be able to navigate using screen readers.

Content SHALL provide:

- semantic headings
- descriptive labels
- accessible tables
- image descriptions
- landmark regions
- meaningful page titles
- announced status updates

Dynamic content SHALL remain understandable.

---

# 6.8 Visual Accessibility

Visual accessibility SHALL include:

- scalable typography
- sufficient color contrast
- responsive layouts
- non-color indicators
- readable spacing
- adjustable zoom
- clear visual hierarchy

Information SHALL never rely solely on color.

---

# 6.9 Hearing Accessibility

Audio and video experiences SHALL provide:

- captions
- subtitles
- transcripts
- visual alerts
- accessible media controls

Essential learning SHALL not depend exclusively on audio.

---

# 6.10 Cognitive Accessibility

Learning experiences SHALL minimize cognitive load.

Representative practices include:

- clear instructions
- consistent layouts
- predictable navigation
- simple language
- progressive disclosure
- distraction reduction
- visual organization

Interfaces SHALL support comprehension.

---

# 6.11 Motor Accessibility

The Academy SHALL support learners with limited motor abilities.

Representative accommodations include:

- larger touch targets
- adequate spacing
- gesture alternatives
- keyboard alternatives
- extended interaction time
- error tolerance

Interaction SHALL remain achievable without precise movements.

---

# 6.12 Inclusive Learning Design

Learning SHALL accommodate diverse learning styles.

Representative methods include:

- text
- images
- diagrams
- video
- audio
- simulations
- interactive exercises
- downloadable resources

Multiple learning formats SHALL be available where practical.

---

# 6.13 Multilingual Learning

The Academy SHOULD support multiple languages.

Language support MAY include:

- translated interfaces
- multilingual content
- multilingual assessments
- subtitles
- localized AI responses

Language selection SHALL remain user-controlled.

---

# 6.14 Localization

Localization SHALL extend beyond translation.

Representative localization includes:

- date formats
- time formats
- numbering systems
- calendars
- currencies (where applicable)
- cultural examples
- regional terminology

Localization SHALL preserve educational meaning.

---

# 6.15 Low-Bandwidth Experience

Learning SHALL remain accessible in limited connectivity environments.

Representative strategies include:

- optimized media
- compressed assets
- adaptive quality
- lightweight pages
- cached resources
- deferred loading

Performance SHALL support diverse network conditions.

---

# 6.16 Offline Learning

Where supported, learners SHOULD be able to:

- download lessons
- access notes
- complete offline activities
- synchronize progress later
- view saved resources

Offline learning SHALL synchronize automatically when connectivity returns.

---

# 6.17 AI Accessibility

AI SHALL improve accessibility through:

- text simplification
- automatic summaries
- speech-to-text
- text-to-speech
- caption generation
- translation assistance
- personalized explanations

AI SHALL complement—not replace—accessible design.

---

# 6.18 Accessibility Testing

Accessibility SHALL be validated using:

- automated testing
- manual testing
- keyboard testing
- screen reader testing
- usability testing
- learner feedback
- accessibility audits

Testing SHALL occur throughout development.

---

Representative accessibility KPIs include:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Accessibility Compliance | Standards compliance |
| Keyboard Success Rate | Keyboard usability |
| Screen Reader Compatibility | Assistive technology support |
| Caption Coverage | Accessible media availability |
| Localization Coverage | Supported languages |
| Accessibility Defects | Reported issues |
| Learner Satisfaction | Accessibility feedback |
| Resolution Time | Accessibility issue response |

Accessibility SHALL be continuously monitored.

---

# 6.20 Accessibility Governance

Accessibility governance SHALL define:

- accessibility ownership
- review procedures
- compliance validation
- accessibility documentation
- issue management
- continuous improvement

Accessibility SHALL remain part of every release process.

---

# 6.21 Validation Checklist

Before publishing learner-facing experiences, the following SHALL be verified:

✓ Keyboard navigation validated

✓ Screen reader compatibility verified

✓ Color contrast reviewed

✓ Captions available

✓ Responsive layouts tested

✓ Localization validated

✓ Accessibility testing completed

✓ Governance approval obtained

---

# 6.22 Governance Integration

Accessibility Standards SHALL integrate with:

- SAFS-03 Component Standards
- SAFS-04 Design Standards
- SAFS-05 Content Standards
- SAFS-06 Governance & Lifecycle Standards
- SAIS-04 Chapters 1–5

Accessibility SHALL be embedded throughout the learning experience lifecycle.

---

# 6.23 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every learner-facing experience SHALL be designed using Universal Design principles.
2. All essential functionality SHALL be accessible via keyboard.
3. Multimedia learning resources SHALL provide captions or equivalent alternatives.
4. Information SHALL never rely solely on color or audio.
5. Learning experiences SHALL support assistive technologies.
6. Accessibility SHALL be validated before every production release.
7. AI-powered accessibility features SHALL remain transparent and user-controlled.
8. Accessibility metrics SHALL be continuously monitored and improved.
9. Accessibility governance SHALL be integrated into product lifecycle management.
10. Accessibility Standards SHALL remain technology, platform, and vendor independent.
