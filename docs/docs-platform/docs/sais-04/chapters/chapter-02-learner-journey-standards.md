# Chapter 2 — Learner Journey Standards

> **Normative**
>
> Every learner journey implemented across Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the Learner Journey Standards defined in this chapter. Every journey SHALL be intuitive, measurable, personalized, accessible, and continuously optimized.

---

# 2.1 Purpose

A learner journey is the complete sequence of experiences a learner has while interacting with the Academy.

A well-designed journey enables learners to:

- discover learning opportunities
- understand their goals
- begin learning quickly
- maintain momentum
- achieve mastery
- continue lifelong learning

Every Academy product SHALL provide a consistent learner journey.

---

# 2.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Journey Architecture
- Discovery Experience
- Registration
- Onboarding
- Goal Setting
- Enrollment
- Learning Sessions
- Progress Tracking
- Assessments
- Certification
- Re-engagement
- Lifelong Learning

---

---

# 2.4 Discovery Experience

The discovery phase SHALL help learners understand the Academy.

Discovery SHOULD include:

- featured courses
- learning paths
- recommendations
- categories
- search
- success stories
- instructor profiles
- AI recommendations

Discovery SHALL minimize friction.

---

# 2.5 Registration

Registration SHALL be simple and secure.

Registration MAY include:

- email
- phone number
- Single Sign-On (SSO)
- enterprise login
- social authentication

Registration SHALL minimize unnecessary data collection.

---

# 2.6 Identity Verification

Where required, identity verification SHALL support:

- certification
- enterprise learning
- compliance
- examination integrity

Verification SHALL respect privacy requirements.

---

Every new learner SHALL complete onboarding.

Canonical onboarding:

```text
Welcome
   │
   ▼
Profile Setup
   │
   ▼
Learning Interests
   │
   ▼
Skill Assessment
   │
   ▼
Goals
   │
   ▼
Personalization

Dashboard Introduction
   │
   ▼
```

Onboarding SHALL prepare learners for success.

---

# 2.8 Learner Profile

Every learner SHALL maintain a profile.

Representative profile information includes:

- learning goals
- interests
- competencies
- achievements
- certifications
- learning preferences
- accessibility preferences

Profile information SHALL support personalization.

---

# 2.9 Goal Setting

Learners SHOULD define learning goals.

Representative goals include:

- career advancement
- certification
- exam preparation
- new skill acquisition
- professional development
- personal interest

Goals SHALL influence recommendations.

---

# 2.10 Personalized Dashboard

After onboarding, learners SHALL receive a personalized dashboard.

The dashboard MAY include:

- continue learning
- recommended courses
- daily goals
- achievements
- progress
- upcoming deadlines
- AI tutor
- notifications
- calendar

The dashboard SHALL become the learner's primary workspace.

---

# 2.11 Learning Discovery

Learners SHALL be able to discover learning through:

- search
- AI recommendations
- categories
- skills
- competency maps
- instructors
- trending courses
- learning paths

Discovery SHALL remain personalized.

---

# 2.12 Enrollment Experience

Enrollment SHALL clearly communicate:

- prerequisites
- learning outcomes
- estimated duration
- certification
- assessments
- difficulty level

Enrollment SHALL require minimal effort.

---

```text
   │
   ▼
```

---

# 2.14 Learning Progress

Progress SHALL be continuously tracked.

Progress indicators MAY include:

- completion percentage
- completed lessons
- competencies
- streaks
- learning time
- assessments
- milestones

Progress SHALL synchronize across devices.

---

# 2.15 Assessment Journey

The assessment experience SHALL include:

- preparation
- instructions
- assessment
- submission
- feedback
- improvement recommendations

Assessment SHALL remain transparent.

---

# 2.16 Completion Journey

When learning is completed, learners SHOULD receive:

- completion confirmation
- achievement summary
- competency updates
- recommendations
- certificate eligibility

Completion SHALL motivate continued learning.

---

---

# 2.18 Community Journey

Learners SHOULD continue engagement through:

- discussions
- study groups
- peer learning
- mentoring
- events
- collaborative projects

Community SHALL encourage long-term participation.

---

# 2.19 Re-engagement Journey

Inactive learners SHOULD receive personalized re-engagement.

Representative strategies include:

- reminders
- recommendations
- AI study plans
- unfinished lessons
- milestone encouragement
- upcoming events

Re-engagement SHALL remain respectful and optional.

---

# 2.20 Lifelong Learning Journey

The learner journey SHALL not end after certification.

Continuous learning MAY include:

- advanced courses
- specialization tracks
- mentoring
- new certifications
- industry updates
- AI recommendations

The Academy SHALL encourage lifelong learning.

---

# 2.21 Journey Analytics

Representative learner journey KPIs include:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Registration Conversion | Visitor to learner |
| Onboarding Completion | Successful onboarding |
| Enrollment Rate | Courses enrolled |
| Course Completion | Courses completed |
| Daily Active Learners | Daily engagement |
| Learning Streak | Consecutive learning days |
| Certification Rate | Certificates earned |
| Retention Rate | Returning learners |

Journey analytics SHALL support continuous optimization.

---

# 2.22 Accessibility

Every learner journey SHALL support:

- keyboard navigation
- screen readers
- captions
- transcripts
- responsive layouts
- localization
- reduced cognitive load

Accessibility SHALL remain integrated throughout the journey.

---

# 2.23 Validation Checklist

Before publishing a learner journey, the following SHALL be verified:

✓ Discovery experience validated

✓ Registration tested

✓ Onboarding completed

✓ Dashboard personalized

✓ Progress tracking operational

✓ Assessments integrated

✓ Accessibility verified

✓ Analytics configured

---

# 2.24 Governance Integration

Learner Journey Standards SHALL integrate with:

- SAFS-01 Academy Architecture
- SAFS-03 Component Standards
- SAFS-04 Design Standards
- SAFS-05 Content Standards
- SAFS-06 Governance & Lifecycle Standards
- SAIS-04 Chapter 1 — Learning Experience Foundation

Journey implementation SHALL remain consistent across all Academy products.

---

# 2.25 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every learner SHALL follow the canonical learner journey architecture.
2. Onboarding SHALL personalize the learning experience.
3. Every learner SHALL have a persistent profile supporting personalization.
4. Learning progress SHALL synchronize across supported devices.
5. Learning sessions SHALL support interruption and seamless resume.
6. Certification journeys SHALL include verifiable credentials.
7. Re-engagement experiences SHALL be personalized and optional.
8. Journey analytics SHALL be continuously measured using standardized KPIs.
9. Accessibility SHALL be integrated into every learner journey.
10. Learner Journey Standards SHALL remain technology, platform, and vendor independent.

- goal setting

- learning discovery
- enrollment
- learning sessions

Together, these standards establish a complete end-to-end learner journey that is personalized, engaging, measurable, inclusive, and designed to support continuous learning throughout every stage of the learner lifecycle.
