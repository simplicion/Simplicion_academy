# Chapter 7 — Personalization & AI Learning Experience Standards

> **Normative**
>
> Every AI-powered and personalized learning experience within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the standards defined in this chapter. AI SHALL enhance learning, improve learner outcomes, and remain transparent, ethical, secure, and learner-controlled.

---

# 7.1 Purpose

Every learner is unique.

Learners differ in:

- knowledge
- goals
- learning speed
- interests
- experience
- accessibility requirements
- preferred learning methods

The Academy SHALL personalize learning experiences to maximize engagement, comprehension, and mastery while ensuring fairness and learner autonomy.

---

# 7.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Personalization Framework
- Learner Modeling
- Adaptive Learning
- AI Tutors
- AI Mentors
- AI Recommendations
- Study Planning
- AI Feedback
- Responsible AI
- Privacy
- Personalization Analytics
- AI Governance

---

# 7.3 Personalization Philosophy

The Academy adopts the following philosophy.

> **Every learner should receive the right learning experience at the right time without losing control over their educational journey.**

Personalization SHALL:

- improve learning outcomes
- reduce unnecessary effort
- respect learner preferences
- remain transparent
- support accessibility
- preserve learner choice

---

---

# 7.5 Learner Model

Every learner SHALL have a continuously evolving learner model.

Representative attributes include:

- goals
- interests
- completed learning
- competencies
- certifications
- preferred learning pace
- preferred content types
- accessibility preferences
- assessment history
- engagement history

The learner model SHALL only contain information necessary to improve learning.

---

# 7.6 Adaptive Learning

Adaptive learning SHALL dynamically adjust based on learner needs.

Adaptation MAY include:

- lesson sequencing
- learning pace
- content difficulty
- assessment complexity
- revision frequency
- prerequisite recommendations
- practice exercises

Adaptation SHALL remain explainable.

---

# 7.7 AI Tutor

The Academy MAY provide an AI Tutor.

Representative capabilities include:

- answering questions
- explaining concepts
- simplifying content
- generating examples
- summarizing lessons
- guiding problem solving
- recommending resources
- generating quizzes

The AI Tutor SHALL encourage learning rather than provide shortcuts that undermine understanding.

---

# 7.8 AI Mentor

The AI Mentor SHOULD support long-term learner development.

Representative responsibilities include:

- goal planning
- progress reviews
- motivation
- career guidance
- study strategies
- learning reflection
- competency planning

The AI Mentor SHALL complement human mentoring where available.

---

# 7.9 Intelligent Recommendations

Recommendations MAY include:

- next lesson
- learning paths
- revision sessions
- practice activities
- assessments
- projects
- community discussions
- mentoring opportunities
- certifications

Recommendations SHALL clearly indicate why they were suggested.

---

# 7.10 Personalized Study Plans

The Academy MAY generate personalized study plans.

Study plans MAY consider:

- learner goals
- available study time
- deadlines
- assessment schedules
- competency gaps
- learning pace

Study plans SHALL remain editable by learners.

---

# 7.11 AI-Assisted Feedback

AI MAY provide feedback on:

- assessments
- coding exercises
- writing assignments
- projects
- presentations
- simulations

Feedback SHALL be:

- constructive
- specific
- actionable
- educational

AI feedback SHALL not replace instructor evaluation where human review is required.

---

# 7.12 Learning Memory

The Academy MAY maintain learning memory for each learner.

Learning memory MAY include:

- completed topics
- misconceptions
- strengths
- improvement areas
- preferred explanations
- previous questions
- revision history

Learning memory SHALL improve continuity across learning sessions.

---

# 7.13 Responsible AI

Every AI feature SHALL comply with responsible AI principles.

AI SHALL be:

- transparent
- explainable
- fair
- accountable
- privacy-preserving
- secure
- human-centered

Learners SHALL know when they are interacting with AI.

---

# 7.14 Learner Control

Learners SHALL remain in control of personalization.

Learners SHOULD be able to:

- modify learning goals
- disable personalization where practical
- review recommendations
- manage preferences
- control notifications
- review stored learning data

Personalization SHALL assist rather than dictate learning.

---

# 7.15 Privacy & Data Protection

Personalization SHALL respect privacy.

Personal data SHALL be:

- minimized
- securely stored
- appropriately retained
- protected from unauthorized access
- governed by Academy policies

Sensitive learner information SHALL not be used beyond its intended educational purpose.

---

# 7.16 AI Accessibility

AI SHOULD improve accessibility through:

- simplified explanations
- translation
- caption generation
- text-to-speech
- speech-to-text
- adaptive interfaces
- personalized accessibility recommendations

Accessibility improvements SHALL remain optional and learner-controlled.

---

# 7.17 Human Oversight

Human oversight SHALL remain available for:

- certification decisions
- grading where required
- learner disputes
- policy enforcement
- academic integrity investigations
- AI error correction

AI SHALL support—not replace—appropriate human judgment.

---

# 7.18 Personalization Analytics

Representative KPIs include:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Recommendation Acceptance Rate | Accepted AI recommendations |
| Learning Goal Completion | Achievement of learner goals |
| Adaptive Learning Success | Improved learner performance |
| AI Tutor Satisfaction | Learner feedback |
| Study Plan Completion | Completion of personalized plans |
| AI Interaction Rate | AI engagement |
| Retention Improvement | Learner retention |
| Learning Outcome Improvement | Educational effectiveness |

Analytics SHALL evaluate educational impact rather than AI usage alone.

---

# 7.19 AI Governance

AI governance SHALL define:

- AI ownership
- model evaluation
- content validation
- recommendation quality
- fairness reviews
- privacy reviews
- security reviews
- performance monitoring
- version management
- continuous improvement

Governance SHALL ensure trustworthy AI experiences.

---

# 7.20 Validation Checklist

Before deploying AI-powered learning features, the following SHALL be verified:

✓ Personalization objectives defined

✓ Learner controls implemented

✓ AI recommendations validated

✓ Privacy review completed

✓ Accessibility reviewed

✓ Human oversight established

✓ Analytics configured

✓ Governance approval obtained

---

# 7.21 Governance Integration

Personalization & AI Learning Experience Standards SHALL integrate with:

- SAFS-02 Data Standards
- SAFS-03 Component Standards
- SAFS-04 Design Standards
- SAFS-05 Content Standards
- SAFS-06 Governance & Lifecycle Standards
- SAIS-04 Chapters 1–6

Every AI-powered learning experience SHALL comply with Academy governance standards.

---

# 7.22 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every personalized learning experience SHALL prioritize learner success.
2. AI recommendations SHALL be explainable and transparent.
3. Learners SHALL retain meaningful control over personalization settings.
4. Adaptive learning SHALL be based on measurable educational needs.
5. AI Tutors SHALL reinforce learning rather than replace learner effort.
6. AI systems SHALL comply with responsible AI principles.
7. Personal learner data SHALL be protected according to Academy governance policies.
8. Human oversight SHALL remain available for high-impact educational decisions.
9. AI effectiveness SHALL be measured using standardized educational KPIs.
10. Personalization & AI Learning Experience Standards SHALL remain technology, platform, and vendor independent.
