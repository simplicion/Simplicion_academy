# SAIS-04 — Learning Experience (LX) Standards

> **Defines the canonical learner experience for every Simplicion Academy product, ensuring that every platform provides a consistent, engaging, accessible, AI-powered, and measurable learning journey.**

Every interface, learner flow, dashboard, interaction, AI feature, and digital experience within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with SAIS-04.

---

## Purpose

Define the canonical learner experience for every Simplicion Academy product, serving as the UX constitution for Simplicion Academy. Every screen, learner flow, dashboard, interaction, AI feature, and future web or mobile experience SHALL conform to these standards, ensuring a consistent experience across all Academy products.

## Primary User Personas

- **Guest**: Unauthenticated visitor exploring learning offerings.
- **Learner / Student**: Active learner gaining skills and completing tracks.
- **Professional**: Self-directed adult learner focusing on career growth and certifications.
- **Instructor**: Content creator and course supervisor managing cohorts.
- **Mentor**: Academic guide providing 1-on-1 assistance and reviews.
- **Organization Admin**: Corporate or institutional administrator managing seats and compliance.
- **Academy Administrator**: Platform administrator managing operational governance.
- **AI Tutor**: Autonomous learning assistant guiding practice and explanations.
- **AI Mentor**: AI system monitoring progress, motivation, and learning plans.

---

## Core Experience Pillars

1. **Simplicity** — Frictionless, focused interfaces without cognitive clutter.
2. **Consistency** — Uniform interactions, terminology, and visual language.
3. **Personalization** — Adaptive learning journeys tailored to individual goals and speed.
4. **Accessibility** — Fully inclusive design adhering to WCAG 2.2 AAA standards.
5. **Discoverability** — Effortless content search, browsing, and AI recommendation.
6. **Engagement** — Meaningful interactive experiences and active learning.
7. **Motivation** — Gamified achievements, feedback loops, and progress momentum.
8. **Collaboration** — Peer interaction, community forums, and mentored learning.
9. **AI Assistance** — Contextual, explainable, and responsible AI tutoring.
10. **Continuous Improvement** — Data-informed experience refinements.
