# Chapter 5 — Risk, Compliance & Audit

> **Normative**
>
> Every Academy asset, organizational process, educational resource, software platform, AI capability, repository, operational procedure, and governance activity SHALL comply with the Risk, Compliance & Audit standards defined in this chapter.

---

# 5.1 Purpose

Every organization faces operational, technical, educational, security, legal, and strategic risks.

The purpose of this chapter is to establish one unified governance framework that enables Simplicion Academy to:

- identify risks
- evaluate impact
- maintain compliance
- verify controls
- conduct audits
- resolve issues
- improve organizational resilience

Risk management SHALL support informed decision-making rather than discourage innovation.

---

# 5.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Risk Management
- Risk Classification
- Compliance Management
- Internal Controls
- Audit Management
- Findings
- Corrective Actions
- Preventive Actions
- Exception Management
- Regulatory Alignment
- Governance Assurance
- Continuous Improvement

---

# 5.3 Risk Philosophy

The Academy adopts the following philosophy.

> **Every significant organizational decision shall consider risk, compliance, and long-term sustainability.**

Risk management SHALL be:

- proactive
- measurable
- transparent
- documented
- continuously monitored

Governance SHALL reduce risk without unnecessarily limiting innovation.

---

# 5.4 Risk Management Architecture

Every identified risk SHALL follow the canonical governance workflow.

```text
Identification
      │
      ▼
Classification
      │
      ▼
  Assessment
      │
      ▼
Prioritization
      │
      ▼
  Mitigation
      │
      ▼
   Approval
      │
      ▼
  Monitoring
      │
      ▼
    Review
      │
      ▼
   Closure
```

Every risk SHALL remain traceable throughout its lifecycle.

---

# 5.5 Risk Categories

Risks SHALL be classified.

Representative categories include:

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Strategic | Organizational direction |
| Educational | Learning quality |
| Technical | Systems and software |
| Security | Information protection |
| Privacy | Personal information |
| Operational | Daily operations |
| Financial | Budget and sustainability |
| Compliance | Standards or regulatory obligations |
| AI | Responsible AI risks |
| Reputational | Organizational trust |

Each identified risk SHALL belong to one or more categories.

---

# 5.6 Risk Identification

Risk identification SHALL occur continuously.

Sources MAY include:

- governance reviews
- operational monitoring
- learner feedback
- audits
- incident reports
- security reviews
- AI evaluations
- compliance assessments

Identified risks SHALL be documented.

---

# 5.7 Risk Assessment

Every identified risk SHALL be assessed.

Assessment SHALL consider:

- likelihood
- business impact
- learner impact
- technical impact
- operational impact
- detectability
- urgency

Assessment criteria SHALL remain consistent across the Academy.

---

# 5.8 Risk Prioritization

Risks SHALL be prioritized using standardized levels.

| Level | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Critical | Immediate action required |
| High | Significant organizational impact |
| Medium | Managed through planned mitigation |
| Low | Routine monitoring |

Prioritization SHALL guide mitigation activities.

---

# 5.9 Risk Register

The Academy SHALL maintain a centralized Risk Register.

Each risk record SHALL include:

- identifier
- title
- description
- category
- owner
- assessment
- mitigation plan
- status
- review schedule
- closure date

The Risk Register SHALL remain version controlled.

---

# 5.10 Mitigation Planning

Every significant risk SHALL define mitigation activities.

Mitigation plans MAY include:

- process improvements
- technical controls
- training
- monitoring
- redundancy
- governance reviews
- documentation updates

Mitigation SHALL reduce organizational exposure.

---

# 5.11 Internal Controls

Internal controls SHALL support governance objectives.

Representative controls include:

- approval workflows
- access control
- version management
- audit logging
- segregation of duties
- repository governance
- automated validation

Controls SHALL remain documented.

---

# 5.12 Compliance Management

Compliance SHALL ensure adherence to:

- Academy Constitution
- Academy Standards
- organizational policies
- contractual obligations
- applicable laws and regulations
- accessibility requirements
- internal governance requirements

Compliance SHALL be measurable.

---

# 5.13 Compliance Reviews

Compliance reviews SHALL verify:

- policy adherence
- documentation
- lifecycle compliance
- metadata completeness
- governance approvals
- audit readiness

Review findings SHALL be documented.

---

# 5.14 Audit Framework

The Academy SHALL conduct governed audits.

Audit categories MAY include:

- Governance Audit
- Educational Audit
- Technical Audit
- Security Audit
- Accessibility Audit
- AI Audit
- Repository Audit
- Operational Audit

Audit scope SHALL be documented before execution.

---

   │
   ▼

   │
   ▼
Evidence Collection
   │
   ▼
Evaluation
   │
   ▼
Findings
   │
   ▼
Reporting
   │
   ▼

   │
   ▼
Verification
   │
   ▼

Audit activities SHALL remain independently reviewable.

---

# 5.16 Audit Evidence

Audit evidence MAY include:

- documentation
- logs
- version history
- approvals
- repository records
- review reports
- metrics
- system records

Evidence SHALL remain authentic and traceable.

---

# 5.17 Findings Management

Audit findings SHALL be classified.

Representative classifications include:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| Critical | Immediate remediation |
| Major | Significant improvement required |
| Minor | Limited corrective action |
| Observation | Improvement opportunity |

Every finding SHALL identify an owner.

---

# 5.18 Corrective Actions

Corrective actions SHALL resolve identified deficiencies.

Each action SHALL define:

- description
- owner
- due date
- verification method
- completion status

Corrective actions SHALL remain traceable.

---

# 5.19 Preventive Actions

Preventive actions SHALL reduce future risk.

Examples include:

- improved standards
- automation
- training
- governance enhancements
- monitoring improvements
- process redesign

Preventive actions SHALL support continuous improvement.

---

# 5.20 Exception Management

Compliance exceptions SHALL be governed.

Every exception SHALL define:

- justification
- owner
- approval
- duration
- mitigation
- review schedule

Expired exceptions SHALL be reviewed before extension.

---

# 5.21 Regulatory Alignment

The Academy SHALL monitor applicable regulatory obligations.

Alignment MAY include:

- educational regulations
- accessibility requirements
- privacy obligations
- intellectual property
- records retention
- organizational policies

Regulatory alignment SHALL be reviewed periodically.

---

# 5.22 Risk Monitoring

Risk monitoring SHALL evaluate:

- unresolved risks
- mitigation effectiveness
- control effectiveness
- compliance status
- audit findings
- recurring issues

Monitoring SHALL support timely intervention.

---

# 5.23 Risk & Compliance Metrics

Representative governance KPIs include:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Open Risks | Active organizational risks |
| Risk Resolution Time | Mitigation effectiveness |
| Compliance Rate | Standards adherence |
| Audit Completion Rate | Audit effectiveness |
| Control Effectiveness | Internal control performance |
| Corrective Action Closure | Issue resolution |
| Repeat Findings | Governance maturity |
| Exception Volume | Governance exceptions |

Metrics SHALL support organizational assurance.

---

# 5.24 Assurance Reporting

Governance assurance SHALL summarize:

- organizational risks
- compliance status
- audit outcomes
- mitigation progress
- outstanding findings
- governance maturity

Assurance reports SHALL support executive decision-making.

---

# 5.25 Validation Checklist

Before governance assurance is completed, the following SHALL be verified:

✓ Risks identified

✓ Risk Register updated

✓ Controls reviewed

✓ Compliance verified

✓ Audits completed

✓ Findings documented

✓ Corrective actions assigned

✓ Governance approved

---

# 5.26 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every significant organizational risk SHALL be documented in the Risk Register.
2. Every identified risk SHALL undergo standardized assessment.
3. Significant risks SHALL define documented mitigation plans.
4. Internal controls SHALL support governance objectives.
5. Compliance SHALL be periodically reviewed.
6. Audit activities SHALL follow the canonical audit workflow.
7. Audit findings SHALL identify accountable owners.
8. Corrective actions SHALL remain traceable until closure.
9. Governance assurance SHALL be supported by measurable KPIs.
10. Risk, Compliance & Audit standards SHALL remain technology, platform, and vendor independent.

---

This chapter establishes the Risk, Compliance & Audit standards for Simplicion Academy.

It defines:

- risk philosophy
- risk architecture
- risk categories
- risk identification
- risk assessment
- risk prioritization
- Risk Register
- mitigation planning
- internal controls
- compliance management
- compliance reviews
- audit framework
- audit process
- audit evidence
- findings management
- corrective actions
- preventive actions
- exception management
- regulatory alignment
- monitoring
- governance metrics
- assurance reporting

Together, these standards ensure that every Academy asset, process, and governance activity is continuously monitored for risk, evaluated for compliance, audited for effectiveness, and improved through structured corrective and preventive actions.
