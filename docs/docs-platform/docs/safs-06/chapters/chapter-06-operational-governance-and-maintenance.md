# Chapter 6 — Operational Governance

> **Normative**
>
> Every operational activity, educational service, digital platform, AI capability, repository, infrastructure component, support function, and business process within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the Operational Governance standards defined in this chapter.

---

# 6.1 Purpose

Operational Governance ensures that the Academy operates reliably, efficiently, securely, and consistently every day.

The purpose of operational governance is to:

- deliver reliable services
- maintain operational quality
- reduce operational risk
- improve organizational efficiency
- support continuous service delivery
- enable measurable operational excellence

Operational governance SHALL apply throughout the Academy ecosystem.

---

# 6.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Operational Governance
- Service Management
- Operational Processes
- Incident Management
- Problem Management
- Operational Monitoring
- Service Continuity
- Capacity Management
- Performance Management
- Operational Documentation
- Continuous Operations
- Operational Improvement

---

# 6.3 Operational Philosophy

The Academy adopts the following philosophy.

> **Every operational process shall be measurable, repeatable, documented, resilient, and continuously improved.**

Operations SHALL support learners, educators, administrators, contributors, and technology platforms without unnecessary interruption.

# 6.23 Validation Checklist

Before an operational process is considered governed, the following SHALL be verified:

✓ Service owner assigned

✓ Operational documentation complete

✓ Monitoring configured

✓ Incident procedures documented

✓ Recovery procedures verified

✓ Capacity evaluated

✓ Operational KPIs defined

✓ Governance approved

---

# 6.25 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every operational service SHALL have an assigned Service Owner.
2. Operational procedures SHALL remain documented and version controlled.
3. Monitoring SHALL be continuously performed for critical services.
4. Every incident SHALL follow the canonical incident workflow.
5. Recurring incidents SHALL undergo Problem Management.
6. Critical services SHALL define continuity and recovery procedures.
7. Operational performance SHALL be measured using standardized KPIs.
8. Operational documentation SHALL remain synchronized with deployed services.
9. Operational reviews SHALL occur periodically to identify improvement opportunities.
10. Operational Governance SHALL remain technology, platform, and vendor independent.
