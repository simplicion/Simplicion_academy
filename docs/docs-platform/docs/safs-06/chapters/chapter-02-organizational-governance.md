# Chapter 2 — Organizational Governance

> **Normative**
>
> Every governance body, organizational unit, leadership role, committee, decision authority, steward, contributor, and operational function within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the Organizational Governance standards defined in this chapter.

---

# 2.1 Purpose

Organizational Governance establishes clear authority, accountability, responsibilities, and decision-making across the Academy.

A well-defined governance structure ensures that:

- decisions are consistent
- responsibilities are clear
- ownership is maintained
- collaboration is effective
- organizational growth remains sustainable

Governance SHALL support both strategic leadership and operational execution.

---

# 2.2 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Governance Organization
- Organizational Structure
- Governance Bodies
- Decision Authority
- Delegation
- Stewardship
- Roles & Responsibilities
- Organizational Communication
- Escalation
- Accountability
- Governance Meetings
- Organizational Documentation

---

# 2.3 Organizational Governance Architecture

The canonical governance organization is:

```text
Academy
   │
   ▼
Governance Board
   │
   ▼
Governance Committees
   │
   ▼
Domain Owners
   │
   ▼
Department Leads
   │
   ▼
Asset Owners
   │
   ▼
Contributors
   │
   ▼
Learners & Users
```

Every governance responsibility SHALL belong to one level within this hierarchy.

---

# 2.4 Governance Bodies

The Academy SHALL establish governance bodies.

Representative governance bodies include:

| Governance Body | Purpose |
|-----------------|---------|
| Governance Board | Strategic governance |
| Academic Committee | Educational oversight |
| Technical Committee | Technology governance |
| Content Committee | Content quality |
| Design Committee | User experience governance |
| AI Committee | Responsible AI oversight |
| Security Committee | Security governance |
| Quality Committee | Quality assurance |

Each governance body SHALL have a documented charter.

---

# 2.5 Governance Board

The Governance Board SHALL serve as the highest governance authority.

Responsibilities include:

- strategic direction
- policy approval
- standards approval
- governance oversight
- organizational priorities
- risk acceptance
- exception approval

Board decisions SHALL be formally recorded.

---

# 2.6 Domain Ownership

Each governance domain SHALL have a designated Domain Owner.

Examples include:

- Education
- Technology
- Data
- AI
- Design
- Content
- Operations
- Security
- Compliance

Domain Owners SHALL remain accountable for standards within their domains.

---

# 2.7 Asset Ownership

Every governed asset SHALL have an assigned owner.

Governed assets include:

- Learning Paths
- Courses
- Components
- APIs
- Policies
- Repositories
- AI Knowledge
- Media
- Documentation
- Design Systems

Ownership SHALL remain current throughout the asset lifecycle.

---

# 2.8 Stewardship Model

Stewardship ensures responsible management of Academy assets.

Every steward SHALL:

- maintain quality
- approve changes
- coordinate reviews
- resolve issues
- preserve standards
- ensure lifecycle compliance

Stewardship responsibilities SHALL be documented.

---

# 2.9 Delegation of Authority

Decision authority MAY be delegated.

Delegation SHALL define:

- delegated authority
- scope
- duration
- limitations
- reporting requirements

Accountability SHALL remain with the delegated authority unless formally reassigned.

---

# 2.10 Decision Authority Matrix

Governance decisions SHALL follow defined authority.

| Decision | Authority |
|----------|-----------|
| Strategy | Governance Board |
| Standards | Domain Owner |
| Content Approval | Content Owner |
| Technical Architecture | Technical Committee |
| Design Approval | Design Committee |
| AI Policy | AI Committee |
| Security Exception | Security Committee |
| Operational Procedures | Operations Lead |

Authority SHALL remain documented.

---

# 2.11 Organizational Responsibilities

Governance SHALL assign responsibilities for:

- planning
- execution
- review
- approval
- monitoring
- reporting
- maintenance
- continuous improvement

Responsibilities SHALL avoid unnecessary overlap.

---

# 2.12 Governance Meetings

Governance meetings SHALL support informed decision-making.

Meeting types MAY include:

- Board Meetings
- Committee Reviews
- Standards Reviews
- Architecture Reviews
- Quality Reviews
- Risk Reviews
- Operational Reviews

Meeting outcomes SHALL be documented.

---

# 2.13 Escalation Framework

Governance SHALL support structured escalation.

Canonical escalation flow:

```text
Contributor
   │
   ▼
Asset Owner
   │
   ▼
Domain Owner
   │
   ▼
Governance Committee
   │
   ▼
Governance Board
```

Escalations SHALL remain traceable.

---

# 2.14 Communication Standards

Governance communications SHALL be:

- timely
- accurate
- transparent
- documented
- accessible
- actionable

Communication channels MAY include:

- documentation
- governance portals
- meetings
- reports
- notifications

---

# 2.15 Organizational Documentation

Every governance body SHALL maintain documentation.

Documentation MAY include:

- charter
- responsibilities
- membership
- operating procedures
- decision records
- meeting minutes
- review schedules

Documentation SHALL remain version controlled.

---

# 2.16 Conflict Resolution

Governance SHALL provide structured conflict resolution.

Conflicts MAY involve:

- ownership
- priorities
- standards interpretation
- resource allocation
- architectural decisions

Conflict resolution SHALL prioritize Academy objectives.

---

# 2.17 Exception Management

Exceptions SHALL be formally managed.

Every exception SHALL include:

- justification
- scope
- owner
- approval
- expiration date (where applicable)
- mitigation plan

Temporary exceptions SHALL be periodically reviewed.

---

# 2.18 Organizational Metrics

Governance SHALL monitor organizational effectiveness.

Representative metrics include:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Ownership Coverage | Assets with assigned owners |
| Decision Cycle Time | Governance responsiveness |
| Meeting Completion | Governance participation |
| Exception Resolution | Governance effectiveness |
| Policy Adoption | Organizational compliance |
| Committee Participation | Governance engagement |
| Escalation Resolution | Issue resolution efficiency |
| Governance Maturity | Organizational capability |

Metrics SHALL support continual improvement.

---

# 2.19 Governance Accountability

Accountability SHALL exist at every governance level.

Every accountable role SHALL:

- accept responsibility
- report progress
- maintain documentation
- manage risks
- support audits
- ensure compliance

Accountability SHALL not be delegated without formal reassignment.

---

# 2.20 Organizational Review

Governance SHALL periodically review:

- organizational structure
- governance bodies
- responsibilities
- committee effectiveness
- delegation models
- stewardship assignments

Reviews SHALL identify opportunities for improvement.

---

# 2.21 Validation Checklist

Before organizational governance is approved, the following SHALL be verified:

✓ Governance bodies established

✓ Roles documented

✓ Owners assigned

✓ Decision authority defined

✓ Delegation documented

✓ Committees operational

✓ Escalation defined

✓ Governance approved

---

# 2.22 Governance Dependencies

Organizational Governance SHALL integrate with:

- Governance Foundation
- Data Governance
- Design Governance
- Content Governance
- AI Governance
- Operational Governance

Dependencies SHALL remain clearly documented.

---

# 2.23 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every governance domain SHALL have a designated Domain Owner.
2. Every governed asset SHALL have an assigned owner.
3. Governance bodies SHALL maintain documented charters.
4. Decision authority SHALL remain formally documented.
5. Delegated authority SHALL define scope and accountability.
6. Governance meetings SHALL produce documented decisions.
7. Exceptions SHALL follow formal exception management.
8. Organizational documentation SHALL remain version controlled.
9. Governance effectiveness SHALL be measured using defined KPIs.
10. Organizational governance SHALL remain technology, platform, and vendor independent.
