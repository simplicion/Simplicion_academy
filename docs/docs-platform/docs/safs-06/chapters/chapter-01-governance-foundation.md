# Chapter 1 — Governance Foundation

> **Normative**
>
> Every organizational process, educational resource, digital asset, software component, policy, repository, AI capability, operational workflow, and governance activity within Simplicion Academy SHALL comply with the Governance Foundation defined in this chapter.

---

# 1.1 Purpose

Governance ensures that every Academy asset is managed consistently throughout its lifecycle.

Without governance:

- ownership becomes unclear
- quality degrades
- duplication increases
- compliance becomes difficult
- organizational knowledge becomes fragmented

The purpose of this chapter is to establish one unified governance model that applies across the entire Academy ecosystem.

Governance SHALL enable:

- accountability
- transparency
- quality
- consistency
- sustainability
- continuous improvement

---

# 1.2 Scope

Governance standards apply to every Academy asset, including:

- Learning Paths
- Tracks
- Modules
- Learning Units
- Assessments
- Components
- Data
- Repositories
- Design Systems
- Content
- AI Knowledge
- Policies
- Documentation
- Software Services
- Digital Assets
- Operational Processes

No Academy asset SHALL exist outside the governance framework.

---

# 1.3 Objectives

This chapter establishes standards for:

- Governance Philosophy
- Governance Architecture
- Governance Principles
- Organizational Roles
- Ownership
- Decision Making
- Accountability
- Lifecycle Governance
- Documentation
- Governance Metrics
- Governance Culture
- Continuous Improvement

---

# 1.4 Governance Philosophy

The Academy adopts the following philosophy.

> **Every organizational asset shall have clear ownership, measurable quality, defined lifecycle, and documented governance.**

Governance exists to:

- protect quality
- support scalability
- enable collaboration
- preserve knowledge
- reduce operational risk
- improve decision making

Governance SHALL support innovation without sacrificing consistency.

---

# 1.5 Governance Principles

Every governance activity SHALL follow these principles.

## Principle 1 — Accountability

Every asset SHALL identify an accountable owner.

---

## Principle 2 — Transparency

Governance decisions SHALL be documented.

---

## Principle 3 — Consistency

Standards SHALL be applied uniformly across the Academy.

---

## Principle 4 — Traceability

Every significant decision SHALL remain traceable.

---

## Principle 5 — Quality

Governance SHALL continuously improve organizational quality.

---

## Principle 6 — Simplicity

Governance SHALL minimize unnecessary complexity.

---

## Principle 7 — Reusability

Governance SHALL encourage reusable assets and processes.

---

## Principle 8 — Continuous Improvement

Governance SHALL evolve based on measurable outcomes.

---

# 1.7 Governance Domains

The Academy governance model consists of the following domains.

| Domain | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Strategic Governance | Organizational direction |
| Educational Governance | Learning quality |
| Technical Governance | Technology standards |
| Data Governance | Data quality |
| Design Governance | User experience |
| Content Governance | Knowledge quality |
| AI Governance | Responsible AI |
| Operational Governance | Daily operations |
| Risk Governance | Risk management |
| Compliance Governance | Regulatory adherence |

Each governance domain SHALL define documented responsibilities.

---

Governance authority SHALL follow a defined hierarchy.

```text
Academy
   │
   ▼
Governance Board
   │
   ▼
Domain Owners
   │
   ▼
Asset Owners
   │
   ▼
Contributors
   │
   ▼
```

Decision authority SHALL be delegated appropriately while maintaining accountability.

Governance responsibilities SHALL be assigned to defined roles.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|

| Domain Owner | Domain oversight |
| Product Owner | Product governance |
| Content Owner | Content quality |
| Technical Lead | Technical governance |
| Design Lead | Design governance |
| AI Lead | AI governance |

| Quality Reviewer | Validation |

---

# 1.10 Ownership Model

Every governed asset SHALL identify:

- owner
- custodian
- contributor
- reviewer
- approver
- maintainer

Ownership SHALL remain valid throughout the asset lifecycle.

---

# 1.11 Decision Framework

Governance decisions SHOULD be based upon:

- organizational objectives
- learner impact
- evidence
- quality metrics
- risk assessment
- compliance requirements
- long-term maintainability

Decision rationale SHALL be documented.

---

# 1.12 Governance Documentation

Every governance activity SHALL be documented.

Documentation MAY include:

- policies
- standards
- procedures
- approvals
- meeting decisions
- review outcomes
- implementation guidance

Documentation SHALL remain version controlled.

---

# 1.13 Governance Lifecycle

Governance SHALL apply throughout the lifecycle.

```text
Proposal
   │
   ▼
 Review
   │
   ▼
Approval
   │
   ▼
Implementation
   │
   ▼
Monitoring
   │
   ▼
Improvement
   │
   ▼
Retirement
```

Each lifecycle transition SHALL be auditable.

---

# 1.14 Policy Management

Policies SHALL define:

- objectives
- applicability
- responsibilities
- compliance requirements
- exceptions
- review schedule
- ownership

Policies SHALL remain centrally governed.

---

# 1.15 Standards Management

Standards SHALL:

- define mandatory requirements
- remain implementation independent
- support interoperability
- remain measurable
- be periodically reviewed

Standards SHALL evolve through governed change management.

---

# 1.16 Governance Metrics

Governance effectiveness SHALL be measurable.

Representative metrics include:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Policy Compliance | Standards adherence |
| Review Completion | Governance efficiency |
| Asset Ownership | Ownership coverage |
| Audit Findings | Governance quality |
| Documentation Coverage | Documentation completeness |
| Lifecycle Compliance | Lifecycle adherence |
| Risk Resolution | Risk mitigation |
| Governance Maturity | Organizational capability |

Metrics SHALL support continuous improvement.

---

# 1.17 Governance Communication

Governance information SHALL be communicated through:

- documentation
- standards
- training
- governance meetings
- announcements
- review reports

Communication SHALL remain transparent.

---

# 1.18 Governance Culture

The Academy SHALL promote a governance culture that values:

- accountability
- collaboration
- openness
- continuous learning
- evidence-based decisions
- quality ownership

Governance SHALL enable—not hinder—innovation.

---

# 1.19 Governance Validation

Before approving governance activities, the following SHALL be verified:

✓ Ownership assigned

✓ Policies referenced

✓ Standards applied

✓ Documentation complete

✓ Lifecycle identified

✓ Responsibilities assigned

✓ Compliance reviewed

✓ Governance approval completed

---

# 1.20 Governance Dependencies

Governance SHALL integrate with:

- SAFS-00 Academy Constitution
- SAFS-01 Academy Architecture
- SAFS-02 Data Standards
- SAFS-03 Component Standards
- SAFS-04 Design Standards
- SAFS-05 Content Standards

Governance SHALL provide the overarching operational framework for all Academy standards.

---

# 1.21 Normative Requirements

The following requirements are mandatory.

1. Every Academy asset SHALL have an identified owner.
2. Governance responsibilities SHALL be formally assigned.
3. Governance decisions SHALL be documented and traceable.
4. Every governed asset SHALL follow a defined lifecycle.
5. Policies and standards SHALL remain version controlled.
6. Governance metrics SHALL be monitored regularly.
7. Governance documentation SHALL remain current.
8. Governance SHALL support continuous organizational improvement.
9. Governance SHALL apply consistently across every Academy domain.
10. Governance standards SHALL remain technology, platform, and vendor independent.
