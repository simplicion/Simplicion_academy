# 8.19 Glossary

## Governance

The framework for directing, controlling, and improving Academy operations.

---

## Lifecycle

The complete sequence of states through which an asset progresses.

---

## Policy

A governing statement defining organizational intent.

---

## Standard

A mandatory requirement defining expected outcomes.

---

## Procedure

A documented sequence of activities for performing work.

---

## Risk

A potential event that could affect organizational objectives.

---

## Audit

An independent evaluation of governance effectiveness and compliance.

---

## Incident

An unplanned event affecting operations or services.

---

## Governance Board

The highest authority responsible for governance oversight.

---

## Continuous Improvement

An ongoing process of enhancing organizational capability through measurable improvements.

# 8.20 Implementation Guidance

Implementation teams SHOULD:

1. Adopt governance incrementally across all Academy domains.
2. Maintain complete governance documentation.
3. Assign ownership before publishing assets.
4. Apply standardized lifecycle management.
5. Measure governance using defined KPIs.
6. Conduct periodic governance reviews.
7. Preserve immutable audit history.
8. Encourage cross-domain collaboration.
9. Continuously improve governance maturity.
10. Maintain compatibility with future Academy standards.

The following requirements are mandatory.
