# Chapter 20 — References & Document History

> **This chapter defines the authoritative references, supporting standards, governance records, document history, approval process, revision management, and maintenance procedures for SAFS-01 — Academy Architecture. It ensures long-term traceability, accountability, and maintainability of the Academy Architecture Standard.**

---

# 20.1 Purpose

This chapter establishes the official reference framework and document history for SAFS-01.

It ensures every architectural decision can be traced to an approved source and every revision is documented throughout the lifecycle of the standard.

---

# 20.2 Scope

This chapter governs:

- Normative References
- Informative References
- Related SAFS Documents
- Supporting Standards
- Architecture Decision Records
- Version History
- Approval Records
- Change Log
- Review Schedule
- Document Lifecycle

---

# 20.3 Reference Categories

References are organized into the following categories.

| Category | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Normative | Mandatory standards that SHALL be followed |
| Informative | Supporting guidance and best practices |
| Internal | Simplicion Academy documentation |
| External | Industry standards and frameworks |
| Historical | Previous document versions and archived material |

Only approved references SHALL be considered authoritative.

---

The following documents are normative for SAFS-01.

# 20.5 Informative References

Informative references provide supporting guidance.

Examples include:

- Educational design methodologies
- Learning science research
- Accessibility guidance
- Information architecture practices
- Knowledge management practices
- AI engineering guidance
- Documentation best practices

Informative references do not override Foundation Standards.

---

# 20.7 External References

External references may include:

- International standards
- Accessibility standards
- Educational frameworks
- Documentation standards
- Information architecture guidance
- Software architecture practices
- AI governance frameworks

External references supplement—but never replace—Foundation Standards.

---

# 20.8 Architecture Decision Records

Major architectural decisions SHOULD be documented using ADRs.

Each ADR should include:

- ADR ID
- Title
- Status
- Context
- Decision
- Alternatives
- Consequences
- Decision Owner
- Approval Date
- Related Standards

ADRs preserve architectural rationale for future reference.

---

# 20.9 Versioning Policy

SAFS follows Semantic Versioning.

| Version Type | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| Major | Breaking architectural changes |
| Minor | Backward-compatible additions or enhancements |
| Patch | Corrections, clarifications, editorial improvements |

Every published version SHALL have a unique version identifier.

---

# 20.10 Revision History

Revision history records all approved changes.

| Version | Date | Summary | Approved By |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| 1.0.0 | Initial Release | First official publication of SAFS-01 | Foundation Authority |

No revision SHALL be removed from the historical record.

---

# 20.11 Change Log

Every revision SHALL document:

- Changed sections
- Added content
- Removed content
- Modified relationships
- Metadata updates
- Compatibility considerations
- Migration guidance (if applicable)

Changes SHALL be traceable to an approved decision.

---

# 20.12 Approval Record

Publication requires formal approval.

Minimum approval record includes:

- Document ID
- Version
- Approval Status
- Approval Date
- Approving Authority
- Review Committee
- Effective Date

Only approved versions SHALL be published.

---

# 20.13 Document Lifecycle

SAFS documents follow the standard lifecycle.

```text
Draft
      │
      ▼
Review
      │
      ▼
Approved
      │
      ▼
Published
      │
      ▼
Maintained
      │
      ▼
Revised
      │
      ▼
Deprecated
      │
      ▼
Archived
```

Lifecycle status SHALL be reflected in document metadata.

---

# 20.14 Review Schedule

SAFS-01 SHALL undergo periodic review.

Recommended schedule:

| Review Type | Frequency |
|-------------|-----------|
| Editorial Review | Every 6 Months |
| Technical Review | Every 6 Months |
| Governance Review | Every 12 Months |
| Major Architecture Review | As Required |

Additional reviews may occur following significant architectural changes.

---

# 20.15 Archiving Policy

Superseded versions SHALL be archived.

Archive records should preserve:

- Original document
- Metadata
- Version number
- Approval record
- Change log
- Retirement reason

Archived versions SHALL remain available for audit purposes.

---

# 20.16 Document Ownership

Every document SHALL identify:

- Owner
- Maintainer
- Review Committee
- Approval Authority
- Contact Point

Ownership SHALL remain current throughout the document lifecycle.

---

# 20.17 Traceability

Every architectural requirement SHOULD be traceable.

Traceability links may include:

- Foundation Standards
- Architecture chapters
- Metadata definitions
- Governance policies
- Repository locations
- ADRs
- Version history

Traceability supports governance, audits, and continuous improvement.

---

# 20.18 Architectural Constraints

The document management process SHALL satisfy the following constraints.

- Approved references only.
- Canonical document identifiers.
- Version-controlled revisions.
- Complete approval records.
- Permanent revision history.
- Scheduled reviews.
- Archived historical versions.
- Foundation Standards alignment.

---

# 20.19 Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Referencing obsolete standards.
- Publishing without approval.
- Missing revision history.
- Incomplete change logs.
- Broken document references.
- Untracked architectural decisions.
- Removing historical versions.

---

# 20.20 Summary

This chapter establishes the official framework for references, approvals, versioning, traceability, and document lifecycle management within SAFS-01.

By standardizing normative references, revision history, change management, approvals, ownership, and archival practices, Simplicion Academy ensures that the Academy Architecture remains authoritative, transparent, maintainable, and continuously governed.

All future revisions of SAFS-01 SHALL comply with the document management framework defined in this chapter.
